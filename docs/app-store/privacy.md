# Privacy Policy for PeriMedi

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This is a draft for App Store use. It describes how PeriMedi handles data. It is not legal advice.

Operator: Harald Reinmüller. Contact: [GitHub issues](https://github.com/rmh78/perimedi/issues).

## What PeriMedi is

PeriMedi is an iPhone app for logging HRT doses, periods, and symptoms during perimenopause. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

## No PeriMedi server

There is no PeriMedi account and no PeriMedi backend. We do not run analytics, ads, or tracking. We do not sell data.

## What is stored

On your iPhone (SwiftData), the app may store:

- Medications, schedules, and dose taken/skipped logs
- Dose and schedule change history (including a name snapshot if you delete a medication)
- Periods and cycle settings
- Symptom scores you choose to enter (untouched scores are not stored)
- Optional day notes
- Language preference and reminder settings

## iCloud

If iCloud is available for the same Apple ID, the app may sync that data through Apple iCloud / CloudKit so another of your devices can see it. Apple processes that sync under Apple’s terms. PeriMedi does not operate a separate cloud.

Second-device restore is still being hardened. Do not rely on iCloud as your only backup. Use More → Backup → Export.

## Export

You can export a JSON file from More. You choose where that file goes (Files, Mail, and so on). Once you share it, that copy is outside the app.

## Notifications

Dose reminders are local notifications on your device. They are not sent through a PeriMedi server.

## Children

PeriMedi is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. The current text will live at this URL once published.
